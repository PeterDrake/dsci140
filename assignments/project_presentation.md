# Overview
During what would be the final exam period, present your project to the class.

Your presentation should last 15 minutes. The target audience is other students in this class.

**This is a team assignment. When you are done:**
* ***One* member of your team should submit the files, including the names of everyone who contributed.**
* **Everyone else should turn in an empty assignment on Google Classroom, including a comment about who turned in the document for your team.**

# Presentation
The checklist below covers what to include in your presentation:

1. [ ] Use slides such as Powerpoint or Google slides.
2. [ ] Introduce yourselves!
3. [ ] Explain what your research questions are. Include context to motivate why the viewer should care.
4. [ ] Clearly explain the variables that you chose to analyze to answer your questions.
5. [ ] Explain what answer was founds.
6. [ ] Present and explain your charts. (Take time to do this slowly and thoroughly!)
7. [ ] Show how your charts supports your answer.
8. [ ] Explain steps in your data processing method, from download to final results. Please don’t show code; instead, talk the viewer through what you did. A diagram such as a flow chart might be helpful here.
9. [ ] Explain the limitations in your methods.
10. [ ] Be sure each team member speaks approximately the same amount of time.
11. [ ] If you were to continue this project, what further questions would you look into? Consider sketching how you would address one of those questions.
12. [ ] At the end include a slide of credits. That is, include the names of your team members as well as the sources where you got your information.
13. [ ] Presentations that are shorter than 13 minutes or longer than 17 minutes do not fulfill the requirements of this assignment. Practice!
