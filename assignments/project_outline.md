# Plots
We will have talked some about visualization in Python and pandas before this assignment is due. You can also peek ahead to chapter 11 of the textbook.

You will need at least three plots related to your data. Some things to consider:
* Are your visualizations clear and easy to understand?
* Are axes, units, etc. clearly labeled?
* Will a viewer who wasn't working with you on your project be able to understand what each plot says?
* For each plot:
  * What does each mark (dot, bar, line, etc.) represent?
  * What does the position, color, shape, or size of each mark represent?
  * What do the horizontal and vertical axes represent?
  * What are the units in which the data are measured?
  * What do you notice, including trends and anomalies?

# Outline
Outline your team's final paper, which will eventually be 6-8 pages long. The paper should **pose questions and answer them using data**. The intended audience is "other students in this class".

Things to consider:
* What background does the reader need?
* What are your research questions? What are your answers and how do the data support these answers?
* What additional questions do you have about your topic? Can you further explore your data (or find additional data) to address these questions?
* What claims are you making? What story are you telling?
* Suppose a skeptical viewer wants to poke holes in your argument. How can you preemptively respond?
