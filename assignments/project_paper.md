# Overview

This is the fifth part of the team project. You will also present your project to the class during what would be the final exam period.

**This is a team assignment. When you are done:**
* ***One* member of your team should submit the files, including the names of everyone who contributed.**
* **Everyone else should turn in an empty assignment on Google Classroom, including a comment about who turned in the document for your team.**

# Paper
Your team's final paper should be 6-8 pages long. The paper should **pose questions and answer them using data**. The intended audience is "other students in this class".

Things to consider:
* What background does the reader need?
* What are your research questions? What are your answers and how do the data support these answers?
* What additional questions do you have about your topic? Can you further explore your data (or find additional data) to address these questions?
* What claims are you making? What story are you telling?
* Suppose a skeptical viewer wants to poke holes in your argument. How can you preemptively respond?
